# Privacy

Coaster Golf is a free puzzle game. It has no accounts, no emails, no cookies and no ad tracking. This page says exactly what it keeps.

## What we store

Only when you put a win on a leaderboard:

- the nickname you choose
- your equation and its score
- the level, and when you submitted it

Leaderboards are public, so anyone can see your nickname, score and equation. Today's daily equations stay hidden until the day ends (midnight UTC).

## What stays on your device

Your progress, best scores, daily streak, nickname, theme and sound setting are saved in your browser's storage. They never leave your device unless you submit to a leaderboard. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

## What we don't collect

- no accounts, emails or passwords
- no cookies
- no ads or ad tracking
- no IP addresses saved: to stop spam, the leaderboard counts submissions per IP address for a minute, and never stores it

## Age

You must be 13 or older to submit to a leaderboard.
